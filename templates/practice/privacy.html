Trust & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. Learn how your data is handled on our self-hosted learning platform.

1. Self-Hosted Infrastructure Guarantee

English Practice is a fully self-hosted educational platform. All course materials, diagnostic test histories, XP gamification progress, and temporary session tokens reside directly on our private self-hosted servers. Your data does not leave our secured perimeter for analytics, tracking brokers, or third-party behavioral profiling.

2. AI Features & Third-Party Processing (OpenRouter)

Certain interactive features—such as our automated IELTS Writing task evaluation, linguistic grammar diagnostic feedback, and sentence variety assessments—utilize artificial intelligence powered by OpenRouter.

When you submit essays or diagnostic prompts for AI assessment, the text is transmitted securely to OpenRouter API endpoints to generate evaluations. No personal identifiers (such as passwords or account emails) are attached to these requests. Processing by OpenRouter is governed by their policies:

  • OpenRouter Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter Terms of Service

3. Information Collected

  • Session Data: Minimal session cookies to track test completion, streaks, and current lesson positions.
  • Diagnostic Answers: Multiple choice selections and grammar test submissions stored to compute score reports.
  • Device & Access Logs: Standard HTTP request logs (IP address, user agent) kept temporarily for security and rate-limiting.

4. Data Retention and Erasure

Because the application is self-hosted and privacy-first, session cookies expire automatically. You can clear your learning history and local cookies at any time directly through your web browser settings.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data requests, or questions regarding this self-hosted deployment, please contact us directly at nevatalapp@gmail.com.
